Product Details

Our Premium Tulsi Kanthi Mala is hand-strung from sacred tulsi wood, sourced responsibly and knotted bead-by-bead by skilled artisans. Each mala is ritualistically cleansed before dispatch, carrying pure and grounded energy into your daily practice — whether for japa meditation, everyday wear, or quiet reflection.
  • 108 hand-knotted tulsi wood beads plus guru bead

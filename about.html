How It Began

A promise made in a forest, kept in every bead.

VEDIS was founded after our founder spent a season living close to nature — waking with the sun, sitting by rivers, and relearning the slow rhythm of ritual. What came back was a simple realisation: modern life had stripped away the pause, the breath, the intention.

We started with a single tulsi mala, hand-strung on a kitchen table, gifted to friends who asked to return to themselves. Word spread quietly. Today, VEDIS is a small studio of artisans who still knot every bead by hand — because devotion cannot be rushed, or automated.
